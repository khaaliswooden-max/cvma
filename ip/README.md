# ip/ — Intellectual Property workstream

Three documents in this directory, intended to be read in order:

1. **`prior-art-survey.md`** — A survey of relevant USPTO patents, applications, academic literature, and open-source systems that establish the state of the art around CVMA's claim space.
2. **`patent-ideation.md`** — Candidate non-obvious patent concepts derived by mapping CVMA's architecture against the prior-art survey to find white space.
3. **`provisional-patent-application.md`** — A draft Provisional Patent Application (PPA) covering the concepts selected for filing.

## Important caveats

- **Not legal advice.** These documents are engineering work product. Anything
  you intend to file at the USPTO must be reviewed by a registered patent
  attorney or agent.
- **Public disclosure clock.** Under 35 U.S.C. § 102, public disclosure starts
  a one-year clock to file in the United States. Outside the US, most
  jurisdictions are absolute novelty. Treat this repo as confidential until
  filing.
- **Inventor declarations** are not included here; they are added when the
  Provisional cover sheet (SB/16) is executed.
- **Patentable subject matter.** Software-implemented systems remain
  patentable under *Alice* when they are tied to specific technical
  improvements; the architecture below is framed accordingly (concrete
  components, signed evidence flows, substrate attestations).
